STAKEHOLDER MAP

JAOF FOODS – Small Shop Stock and Sales Tracker

1. Stakeholder Identification

The stakeholders are grouped into three rings according to their level of involvement in the business and the proposed system.

Ring 1: Primary Stakeholders

These are the people who will directly use or manage the system.

No.| Stakeholder| Interest / Responsibility
1| Business Owner / Manager| Oversees business activities, stock and sales.
2| Fish Sales Recorder| Records fish sales and monitors quantities sold.
3| Production / Laboratory Worker| Handles food production and monitors materials used.
4| Shop Attendant| Handles customer purchases and daily shop sales.
5| Fish Pond Manager / Worker| Monitors fish farming, harvesting and fish quantities.

Ring 2: Secondary Stakeholders

These people support business operations or are affected by the system's activities.

No.| Stakeholder| Interest / Responsibility
6| Suppliers| Supply raw materials, ingredients and other products.
7| Customers| Purchase food products and fresh or smoked fish.
8| Inventory / Storekeeper| Helps monitor stored materials and finished products.
9| Accountant / Finance Officer| Uses sales and financial records to monitor business performance.
10| Production Supervisor| Oversees production activities and product quality.

Ring 3: External Stakeholders

These stakeholders are not expected to use the system regularly but may influence or benefit from its operation.

No.| Stakeholder| Interest / Responsibility
11| System Developer| Develops, tests and maintains the system.
12| System Administrator / IT Support| Manages system access, backups and technical issues.
13| Regulatory / Food Safety Authorities| Have an interest in food safety and business compliance.
14| Delivery Personnel| May support the movement of products to customers.

2. Demands and Fears of the Top Three Stakeholders

Stakeholder 1: Business Owner / Manager

Demands:

- Accurate information about available stock.
- Easy access to sales and production reports.
- Better monitoring of raw materials and finished products.
- A system that is simple and reliable.

Fears:

- Loss of important business records.
- Incorrect stock and sales information.
- Unauthorized access to business information.
- A system that is difficult to operate.

Stakeholder 2: Fish Sales Recorder

Demands:

- An easy way to record fish sales.
- Separate tracking of fresh and smoked fish.
- Automatic calculation of sales totals.
- Updated information about available fish quantities.

Fears:

- Losing sales records.
- Entering incorrect quantities or prices.
- Difficulty using the system.
- Stock information not updating after a sale.

Stakeholder 3: Production / Laboratory Worker

Demands:

- Easy recording of raw materials and ingredients.
- Ability to record production activities.
- Monitoring of finished product quantities.
- Information about materials that are running low.

Fears:

- Incorrect records of materials used.
- Losing production information.
- Difficulty tracking finished products.
- Delays caused by inaccurate stock information.

3. Stakeholder Summary

The business owner, fish sales recorder and production worker are the most important stakeholders because their activities are directly related to the main functions of the proposed system.

The system should therefore be designed to support stock management, fish sales, food production and reporting while remaining simple enough for daily use.
